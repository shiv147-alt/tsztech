Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about cloud solutions answered

What happens to our current email data?

Nothing is lost. We migrate all emails, contacts, and calendar entries to Exchange Online. We verify every item before switching over.

Do we need to change our email address?

No. We keep your domain and email addresses intact. Your customers and partners see no change in your email addresses.

Is Microsoft 365 secure?

Yes. Microsoft invests billions annually in security. Exchange Online includes advanced threat protection, encryption, and compliance certifications.

What's included in the price?

All plans include Exchange Online, Teams, OneDrive, and web versions of Office apps. Premium plans add desktop Office apps and advanced security.

Can we integrate with existing business applications?

Absolutely. Microsoft 365 integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, ERP systems, and thousands of other business apps through Power Automate and APIs.

How much will migration cost?

Costs depend on your organization size and complexity. We provide detailed quotes after assessment. Most businesses see ROI within 6-12 months.
